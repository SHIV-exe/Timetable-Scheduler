Analytical Findings (Section 11.5)

On the small dataset, Backtracking with Heuristics solved in under 0.05 seconds with only 50–80 node visits. However, as problem size scaled to the large dataset (144 sessions across 8 classes), Plain Backtracking timed out completely on 10/10 runs due to combinatorial tree explosion. Min-Conflicts proved to be the fastest on large problems, finding clash-free solutions in under 0.5s via O(1) incremental counter lookups.
Systematic backtracking is indifferent to soft constraints and terminates at the first clash-free leaf, leading to high soft penalty scores (~250–320). In contrast, Simulated Annealing and the Hybrid Solver (BT + SA) produced the best timetable ergonomics, reducing soft penalty scores by over 50% while preserving zero hard violations.
Backtracking CSP (Heuristics) is indispensable when mathematical proof of unsatisfiability is required or for tightly constrained cohorts. Min-Conflicts is optimal for fast, near-instant generation in large enterprise scenarios. The Hybrid Solver represents the gold standard when both zero violations and faculty ergonomics (minimal gaps and low fatigue) are non-negotiable.
